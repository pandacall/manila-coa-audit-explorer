# COA Annual Audit Reports — City of Manila, 2020–2024

Raw source documents for COA Audit Explorer: the Commission on Audit's (COA) Annual Audit Reports (AARs) on the City of Manila for calendar years 2020–2024, as published by COA.

- **Source:** Commission on Audit, https://www.coa.gov.ph (Annual Audit Reports → Local Government Units → NCR → City of Manila)
- **Obtained:** downloaded manually by the project owner, on or before 2026-10-01
- **Layout:** one folder per year, files numbered as COA publishes them (01 Transmittal Letter … 11 Annexes). From 2023, the AAR files sit in `AAR/` and the Agency Action Plan and Status of Implementation (AAPSI) and Action Plan Monitoring Tool (APMT) sit in `AAPSI_APMT/`.
- **`_duplicates/`:** PDF renderings of the 2020–2023 Executive Summaries, also published by COA. They duplicate each year's `03-…Executive_Summary` file and are not indexed.

These files are committed unmodified so the project is reproducible even if COA's links change. They are public government documents; this project is independent and not affiliated with COA.
